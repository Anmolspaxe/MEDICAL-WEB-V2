1. Who We Are

MediCare General Hospital ("we", "us", "our") is a healthcare provider operating at Sector 5, Vaishali, Ghaziabad – 201010, Uttar Pradesh, India. We operate the MediCare hospital management system accessible at this website and related mobile applications.

We are a Data Fiduciary as defined under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDPA 2023). Our registered Data Protection Officer (DPO) can be reached at privacy@medicare.hospital.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Personal Data (provided by you)

  • Identity: Full name, age, gender, date of birth
  • Contact: Phone number, email address, residential address
  • Authentication: Hashed password (bcrypt, never plain text), one-time passwords (OTPs)
  • Government ID: ABHA Health ID (optional, for ABDM integration)

2.2 Medical / Sensitive Data

  • Appointment history, doctor names, consultation reasons
  • Prescriptions including diagnoses, medicines, and dosage instructions
  • Medical reports: X-Rays, MRIs, blood tests, ECG, ultrasound images, PDFs
  • Symptoms and clinical notes recorded during consultations

2.3 Technical Data (collected automatically)

  • IP address (logged with every action for security and audit purposes)
  • Browser user-agent and device type
  • Timestamps of all actions (login, booking, payment)
  • Payment transaction IDs (we do not store card numbers — handled by Razorpay PCI-DSS Level 1)

We never collect or store: credit/debit card numbers, CVV codes, bank account numbers, Aadhaar numbers, or biometric data.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process your personal data only for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Providing medical services: booking appointments, issuing prescriptions, uploading reports
  • Sending appointment confirmations, reminders, and status updates via SMS and email
  • Authenticating your identity and securing your account with 2FA
  • Processing consultation fee payments via Razorpay
  • Maintaining a tamper-evident audit trail for medical compliance
  • Complying with MCI/NMC data retention requirements (7-year minimum)
  • Improving our services through aggregate, anonymised analytics

⚠️ We do not use your medical data for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties. We do not use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Data Sharing

We share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

4.1 Within MediCare

Your appointment details are visible to the assigned doctor and reception staff. Medical reports are accessible to the treating doctor and authorised reception staff.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or a competent government authority, including health regulatory bodies (MCI/NMC) and law enforcement agencies.

5. Data Retention

  • Medical records (appointments, prescriptions, reports): minimum 7 years as required by MCI Guidelines 2002
  • Audit logs: minimum 7 years — append-only, cannot be deleted
  • Account data (name, email, phone): retained while account is active + 2 years after last activity
  • Payment records: 8 years as required by the Income Tax Act
  • Consent records: retained for the lifetime of the account

After the retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymised. You may request earlier deletion of non-medical account data — see Your Rights below.

6. Security Measures

  • Encryption in transit: TLS 1.3 for all data between your browser and our servers
  • Encryption at rest: AES-256 via Supabase (AWS)
  • Password security: bcrypt with cost factor 12 — passwords are never stored in plain text
  • Two-factor authentication: SMS OTP and TOTP (Google Authenticator) available for all roles
  • Role-based access: Patients see only their own data; doctors see only their patients; Row Level Security enforced at database level
  • Audit trail: Every login, data access, and change is recorded in a tamper-evident log
  • Rate limiting: Authentication attempts limited to prevent brute-force attacks

8. Consent & Withdrawal

When you create a patient account, we obtain your free, informed, specific, and unambiguous consent before collecting your data. The exact version of the policy you consented to, along with the timestamp and your IP address, is recorded in our systems.

You may withdraw consent at any time by emailing privacy@medicare.hospital. Withdrawal does not affect the legality of processing done before withdrawal. However, withdrawal may limit our ability to provide medical services.

9. Cookies & Session Storage

Our web application uses browser sessionStorage (not cookies) to maintain your login session. SessionStorage data is cleared automatically when you close your browser tab.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics scripts.

10. Children's Data

We provide paediatric services. When a patient is under 18 years of age, we require a parent or legal guardian to provide consent on their behalf during registration. We do not knowingly collect data from minors without verifiable parental consent.

11. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email and/or an in-app notice at least 30 days before the changes take effect. Continued use of our services after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

The version identifier (e.g. privacy_v1_2025) in the consent record allows us to track which version of this policy you accepted.
