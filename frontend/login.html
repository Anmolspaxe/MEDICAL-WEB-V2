Data Privacy & Consent

Please read and accept our data privacy policy before registering.

What data we collect

Name, contact info, age, gender, appointment history, medical reports, and prescriptions.

How we use it

Solely for providing medical services at MediCare Hospital. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Your rights (DPDPA 2023)

You have the right to access, correct, erase your data, and withdraw consent at any time by writing to privacy@medicare.hospital.

Data retention

Medical records retained for 7 years as required by MCI guidelines. Non-medical account data can be deleted on request.

Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Access controlled by role-based permissions with full audit logging.
